### Priority Issues

- **[P0] Aesthetic Mismatch (Brand & Trust)**
  - **Why it matters**: A traditional B2B client looking for reliable printer maintenance won't trust a site that looks like a crypto dashboard.
  - **Fix**: Re-theme the entire application to the requested Blue and White palette. Remove all cyan glows and dark mode backgrounds.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize` (or a full redesign)

- **[P1] "AI-Tell" Typography & Clutter**
  - **Why it matters**: Gradient text (`bg-clip-text bg-gradient-to-r`) and pinging/pulsing animations distract from the business value.
  - **Fix**: Use solid, high-contrast text for headlines. Remove decorative gradients and unnecessary CSS animations.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

- **[P1] Contrast & Accessibility Failures**
  - **Why it matters**: 14 instances of `text-slate-950 on bg-cyan-400`. This washes out primary actions and makes them hard to read.
  - **Fix**: Use standard, high-contrast button combinations (e.g., white text on dark blue background).
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`
